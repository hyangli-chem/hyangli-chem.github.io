Synthesis, Reactivity, and Aromaticity of a Metallaindacyne

金属杂吲哒炔的合成、反应性及芳香性分析

Li Hongyang, Binbin Xu, Zhengyu Lu, Dafa Chen*, and Haiping Xia*

Abstract details

Registered abstract title

Synthesis of Metallaindacene/yne and Density Functional Theory (DFT) Calculations

金属杂吲哒烯/炔的合成与密度泛函理论（DFT）计算

Indacene is a typical 12π-electron system consisting of two five-membered rings fused to a six-membered ring. Its inherent antiaromaticity makes the parent molecule and its derivatives highly reactive and difficult to stabilize. Although indacene and indacyne have been studied for decades, stabilizing this framework at room temperature while maintaining a neutral, fully conjugated structure remains an unresolved challenge.[1]

Following the concept of carbolong chemistry and building on previously synthesized metallapentalenes and metallapentalynes,[2] this study introduces a metal atom at the bridgehead position shared by the six- and five-membered rings of indacyne to obtain a metallaindacyne. The resulting tricyclic metallaindacene/indacyne framework comprises a [6,5] system fused to a cyclopentadiene ring. Density functional theory (DFT) calculations indicate that dπ-pπ conjugation induces an aromaticity reversal from antiaromaticity in the organic parent indacyne to aromaticity in the metallaindacyne.

The [6,5] core is expected to enable the construction of a series of metallaindacene derivatives with [6,5,3], [6,5,4], and [6,5,5] frameworks. As new members of the carbolong family, these compounds are expected to retain the advantageous properties of carbolong compounds based on the [5,5] framework.[3] In addition, the cyclopentadiene ring fused to the [6,5] core is expected to further enhance electron delocalization, potentially leading to narrower band gaps and near-infrared absorption in metallaindacene/indacyne frameworks.

Keywords: Carbolong chemistry; metal complexes; aromaticity
